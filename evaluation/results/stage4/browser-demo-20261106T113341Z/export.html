IncidentLens report

“Customers say that transfer notifications are arriving late.” · service ledgercore-local · window 2026-11-06T11:28:33 to 2026-11-06T11:31:03 UTC

Diagnosis supported in the observed window

Notifications were late because outbox delivery to the consumer stalled for about 100 s. Delivery attempts were refused (CONNECTION_FAILED) while transfers kept committing normally.

Findings

  1. observed Outbox publications stopped for about 100 s (buckets ~1, 0, 0...), then resumed at 80 per 10 s, while arrivals stayed at 32 per 10 s. Pending and oldest age grew to 292 and 116 s.

    Per-bucket counts and the pending and age gauges show the backlog growing during the stall.

    Evidence: E2

  2. observed During the stall, 1546 delivery attempts failed with CONNECTION_FAILED (network_error). Only 333 succeeded and no HTTP errors occurred.

    Failures took about 0.26 ms with no consumer record, which fits refused connections to the consumer target.

    Evidence: E3, E4

  3. observed The transfer API and the database were healthy, so the delay sits in the outbox-to-consumer hop.

    Transfers returned 201 with p95 0.24 s and no 500s. The pool had no timeouts or pending borrowers, and no DB blocking was sampled.

    Evidence: E1, E5, E6

Alternatives considered

  • weakened Database lock or connection pool contention slowed the outbox poller. No pool timeouts or pending borrowers, and no blocking was seen in 75 samples. E5 E6
  • weakened Slow consumer responses caused the delay. Successful calls took about 71 ms, and failures were immediate connection refusals. E4

Limitations

  • The tools do not show why the consumer at 127.0.0.1:18090 refused connections, for example whether it was down or restarting.
  • Only the first 100 of 1879 attempt records are listed, so the exact time delivery resumed comes from bucket counts.

Next checks

  • Check consumer process logs and availability on port 18090 between about 11:29:04 and 11:30:40.
  • Check whether the retry or backoff settings (maxAttempts=8) risk marking events FAILED during a longer outage.
